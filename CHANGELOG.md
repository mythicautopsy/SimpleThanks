# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

First release for WoW Forever.

- Automatically thanks players who buff you, out of combat
- Three ways to say thanks: `/thank` emote, custom emote, or whisper
- Custom message with `%n` (player name) and `%s` (buff name)
- Per-player cooldown and minimum buff duration, so you don't spam anyone
- Option to ignore party and raid members
- Private chat note showing who buffed you
- Options window (`/simplethanks` or `/stb`), also under Esc → Options → AddOns and the minimap addon button
